# Design plans for concepts 31–60 (frontend-design skill)

Each concept was planned before it was built: palette (4–6 hex), type, layout, and the one place it
spends its boldness. Every plan was then checked against the generic defaults the skill warns about
(cream + terracotta, black + acid green, broadsheet hairlines, identical rounded SaaS cards, ALL-CAPS
eyebrow labels, "A · B · C" meta strings, monospace micro-labels, "→" on buttons). Where a plan
drifted toward one, the revision is noted. Copy is sentence case and says what a button does.
