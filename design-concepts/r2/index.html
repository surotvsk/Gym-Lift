GymQuest redesign, round 2

60 new directions for GymQuest. Unlike round 1, these are working prototypes: every design runs the same engine, so you can really train in each one. Start a workout, log sets, change a set’s weight, finish it, and it shows up in the history, calendar, records and XP. That workout then appears in all 60 designs, because they share one sample account stored in this browser.

Each design draws its own home, workout, progress and Community screens. Deeper forms like the plan editor, library, settings and chat open as sheets in that design’s colours and type.
